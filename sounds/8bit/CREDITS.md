# 8bit

Original chiptune effects generated by [`tools/make-8bit.py`](../../tools/make-8bit.py)
with a pulse-wave synth. The melodies were written for feedme; nothing here is
sampled or transcribed from an existing game.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `pipe-down.wav` | three falling "vwoop" pulses |
| `game-over.wav` | a sad chromatic slide down |
| `power-up.wav` | a fast major arpeggio climbing three octaves |
| `jump.wav` | a quick upward chirp |

License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Regenerate with `python3 tools/make-8bit.py sounds/8bit`.
