from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (license checked on
each sound's page), trimmed and loudness-normalised with
[`tools/import-sound.sh`](../../tools/import-sound.sh).
